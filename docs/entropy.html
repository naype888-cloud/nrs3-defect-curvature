The entropy counts the defect, and the defect fixes the curvature

Boltzmann and Planck: the entropy is the logarithm of the number of configurations. On a cut with M crossing links, k quanta give S = k log M. The 2g links of a surface of genus g all cross the cut after site 0, so the surface is one of those configurations, and Gauss–Bonnet turns its defect into curvature:

S(Σ_g) = (log M / 2π) · (4π − ∫K dA)
