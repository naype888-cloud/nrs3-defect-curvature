The defect of transport fixes the curvature

Transport moves on the path, a tree. A link between two sites closes a cycle exactly when the sites are not neighbours, and every cycle carries one quantum δ∞ = C∞ − 1. Two links make one handle: +2δ∞ of defect and, by Gauss–Bonnet, −4π of curvature. The new cycle is felt only inside the light cone, one site per step.
